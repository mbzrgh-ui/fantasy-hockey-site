How this works

Each player's per-game rate blends the Left Wing Lock rest-of-season projection with his actual NHL rate. Actual weight is GP/(GP+k), capped, with a separate k per category: shots, hits and blocks trust actual play quickly, goals and PPP slowly. Goals are shots × shooting %, with shooting % pulled toward the projection. Goalies blend save % by shots faced (k = 2,000).

The rest of the season is simulated game by game on the real NHL schedule: daily lineups within your slots, one starting goalie per NHL game shared across the league, the 3-appearance goalie minimum with streaming, reported injury return dates (ESPN) with defaults when none is reported, then a reseeded 8-team playoff. Completed results are locked.

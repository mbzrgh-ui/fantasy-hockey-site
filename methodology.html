Methodology

Player rates

Skater per-game rate = w × actual NHL rate + (1 − w) × Left Wing Lock rest-of-season rate, where w = min(cap, GP / (GP + k)). Defaults: SOG k=10 (cap 70%), HIT k=8 (75%), BLK k=8 (75%), A k=25 (50%), PPP k=30 (50%). These follow how quickly each stat stabilises: shots, hits and blocks are repeatable early; goals, assists and power-play points need much longer.

Goals = blended shots × blended shooting %, where shooting % = (goals + 250 × projected sh%) / (shots + 250). This keeps a hot or cold shooting streak from driving the forecast.

Goalies: save % = w × actual + (1 − w) × projection, w = shots / (shots + 2,000), capped at 50%. Win rate and shots per start blend by starts. Start share blends LWL's projected starts with actual starts and is normalised within each NHL team.

Simulation

Every remaining NHL game is simulated. Each simulated season draws a talent multiplier per skater (offence and physical separately), a scoring environment per team-game shared by every skater in it, one starting goalie per team-game (so two fantasy goalies from the same club never both start), and Poisson game stats. Lineups are set daily from each roster, filling C/LW/RW/D/G slots with the best available players. Teams under 3 goalie appearances stream replacement starts at .900 save %, 45% win rate (90% of the time for other managers). Your streams are paid for with adds: 69 for the season, minus 12 held for the playoffs, minus adds already used, minus ordinary pickups at your pace so far. What's left buys streams at 2 adds each (the goalie, then a skater back), within the 4-adds-a-week cap. Playoff streams come out of the 12 held back. A team that still misses the minimum forfeits W, GAA and SV%.

Current-week totals from Yahoo are locked in; only the remaining days are simulated. Standings use category wins (each category counts), the top 8 make the playoffs (weeks 24–26), reseeded each round, with ties going to the higher seed.

Injuries

ESPN's estimated return date is used when reported. Otherwise defaults apply: DTD 3 days, O 7, IR 21, IR-NR 42, IR-LT 56. Season-ending placeholders are treated as out for the season. The "Injuries +4 wks" scenario pushes every return back four weeks.

Decisions

Your team is simulated from per-game draws seeded per player, and every roster option faces the identical simulated seasons (common random numbers). That's why a one-point change in odds is meaningful. Waiver options are screened from the best available free agents, each paired with the drop (from your lowest-value non-keepers, and every goalie when you carry three) that costs you least.

Lineup advice weights each stat by how much one more unit moves this week's matchup (the chance the category margin is near zero), so a category you've already won or lost carries little weight.

Known limits

  • Other managers' future pickups and trades aren't modelled, apart from goalie streaming.
  • Waiver claims (you're on a rolling list) aren't modelled; recently dropped players may need a claim.
  • The adds budget and pickup pace are set in config.yaml (adds_reserved_playoffs, adds_per_stream, pickup_adds_per_week). Adds made this week are read from Yahoo's recent transactions, which only list the last few dozen league moves.
  • Roster limits and IR slots aren't enforced: a player returning from IR is counted as active without a matching drop.
  • Odds aren't yet calibrated. The track record section scores each week's predictions as results come in.
